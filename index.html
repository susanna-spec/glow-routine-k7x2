My glow routine

Your stage

Start at 2 nights a week. Move to 3 after about a month if your skin feels comfortable.

    Weekly plan

    Never put Differin and the SOME BY MI toner on the same night.

    Products on the shelf

    Owned but not in the daily rotation yet. Add only one new product every 2 weeks, and patch test first.

    Use now

    Dr.Althea 345 Relief Cream
    Backup moisturizer for any night your skin feels irritated. Good for the reset stage.
    Tower 28 SOS Daily Rescue Spray
    Gentle hypochlorous acid mist that calms redness and breakouts. Built into Differin and rest nights and the Body tab. Also good during the day over makeup or bare skin, though it won't set makeup. A faint pool smell is normal and fades.
    COSRX Hyaluronic Acid Intensive Cream
    Richer than your ceramide cream. Use on Differin nights or in winter, mostly on the cheeks.

    Add later

    Beauty of Joseon Revive Eye Serum
    Contains retinal, so it is another retinoid on top of Differin. Wait until you are settled in the Build stage, then start 2 rest nights a week, around the eyes only.
    COSRX The 6 Peptide Skin Booster Serum
    Gentle peptides for firmness and barrier support. Use it from the Restart stage on, in place of the Mixsoon essence on rest nights. You already know it agrees with your skin, so you can also use it on Differin nights before the first thin layer of moisturizer.
    The Ordinary Lactic Acid 10% + HA
    Optional swap for the SOME BY MI toner if texture hasn't improved after a couple of months. Never use both.

    Hold off

    The Ordinary Glycolic Acid 7% Toner
    Too harsh for sensitive skin and overlaps with your exfoliant. Use it on the body instead (see Body care below).
    MEDITHERAPY Retinal Skin Booster
    Doubles up with Differin on the face. Use it on the body (see Body care below), or keep it as a gentler backup if Differin is too strong.
    Vitapair C PDRN Serum
    Same job as the Goodal serum, and contains fragrance and lemon extract. Patch test if you use it up later.
    Farmacy Honey Halo
    Fine as a winter moisturizer. Skip it if you react to honey or propolis.

    Body care

    Body skin is thicker and less sensitive, so these work well below the neck.

    The Ordinary Glycolic Acid 7% Toner

    Where to use

    • Backs of the arms and thighs
    • Elbows, knees, and heels
    • Underarms, gently
    • Shaved legs and outer bikini line, not within a day of shaving
    • Back (not the chest, which is for the retinal)

    Helps with

    • Rough, bumpy skin on the backs of the arms or thighs
    • Rough elbows, knees, and heels
    • Ingrown hairs and razor bumps
    • Dullness or uneven tone on arms or legs
    • Underarm bumps and darkening from friction or shaving, gradually over weeks to months

    How to use

    • Swipe on with a cotton pad after a shower, on dry skin, 2 to 3 evenings a week
    • Follow with body lotion
    • Not right after shaving or on broken skin
    • Underarms: if it stings, apply deodorant in the morning instead of the same night. Stop if it burns, since irritation can make darkening worse

    MEDITHERAPY Retinal Skin Booster

    Where to use

    • Chest (décolletage)
    • Neck, starting slowly
    • Backs of the hands
    • Belly, hips, or thighs, for early stretch marks
    • Not on underarms or other skin folds

    Helps with

    • Sun damage and uneven tone on the chest, neck, and backs of the hands
    • Crepey or rough texture
    • Early, still-pink stretch marks (modest results)

    How to use

    • Thin layer in the evening, 2 to 3 times a week, then moisturizer
    • Start slowly on the neck and chest, which are more delicate
    • Wash your hands afterward so it doesn't reach your face
    • Start with both 2 nights a week (Tuesday and Friday). After about a month, if skin is comfortable, switch to 3 (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday) using the Body tab
    • Keep them on different areas, such as glycolic on arms, legs, and underarms and retinal on chest, neck, and hands. Then both can be used the same night
    • Avoid both on your face, the genital area and inner bikini line, and broken, sunburned, or freshly shaved skin
    • Wear sunscreen on treated areas that get sun
    • See a doctor for underarm darkening that feels velvety or thickened or is spreading, or for painful, deep, or recurring bumps
    • Retinoids, including Differin, are generally avoided during pregnancy or while trying to conceive. Check with your doctor if that applies

    Rules to remember

    • Move up a stage only when nothing happensIf any irritation lasts more than a day, stay at your current stage or go back one.
    • How to apply DifferinWait until skin is fully dry. Use a pea-sized amount for the whole face, sandwiched between thin layers of moisturizer. Keep it away from the eyes, nose creases, and mouth corners.
    • If it still irritates at once a weekAdapalene may not suit your skin. Try the MEDITHERAPY retinal serum instead. See a dermatologist for burning, swelling, a rash, or lingering flushing.
    • Signs of irritationStinging from hydrating products, tightness, shiny tight-looking skin, flaking, or redness.
    • Sunscreen every morningRetinoids and acids make skin more sun-sensitive. Reapply when outdoors for long periods.
    • If filaments don't improveAfter 6 to 8 weeks, consider swapping the SOME BY MI toner for COSRX BHA Blackhead Power Liquid.
